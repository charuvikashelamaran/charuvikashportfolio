How I work.

Assess the evidence

With RansomWatch, I compare two folder snapshots to see which files appeared, disappeared, or changed size. I can report exactly what changed and explain why identifying the cause takes further investigation.

Give AI a clear brief

When building Car Dodge in MATLAB, I used the player’s experience to guide my prompts. A concrete requirement, such as how the car should respond to a steering input, gives me something I can check in the game.

Design for the customer

Marketing frames and mirrors for my dad taught me what people ask before they buy. I used those questions to shape the Vikash Frame Works website: visitors can browse in Tamil or English, then send a photo to ask about sizing and price.

Keep people informed

As first-semester class representative, I balanced coursework with attendance reporting and faculty coordination. When classmates requested a deadline change, I gathered their concerns and gave the instructor a specific request to act on.
